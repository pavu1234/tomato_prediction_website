Understand the model and its limits

What happens during analysis?

Drone ON starts your device camera after permission. Capture image selects a still photo while keeping the live camera running. Drone OFF stops camera access. Your photo is decoded as RGB and resized to 224 × 224. The trained model returns three scores. The class with the largest score is displayed. Camera photos use exactly the same prediction process as uploads.

The framing overlay is only a visual guide. It does not locate lesions, measure infected area, or crop your photo.

What has actually been measured?

Validation accuracy is 95.68% on 440 curated images, with macro F1 of 0.9566. The 424-image locked test set has not been evaluated. Validation accuracy does not describe the certainty of an individual prediction.

Changing the frontend does not improve the model’s accuracy. Field and aerial drone performance have not been established. Speed depends on your device.

What is virtual here?

The drone artwork and cockpit are a virtual interface. The camera feed comes from your device, and the prediction comes from the actual trained model. There is no GPS, flight control, battery telemetry, or connection to physical aircraft.

Mission inputs are your own notes. They do not affect model scores. The model does not provide pesticide or treatment prescriptions.
