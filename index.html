About this model 95.68% validation accuracy

Trained on PlantVillage

MobileNetV3Small, trained on a curated subset of tomato leaf images. This app uses the original float32 TensorFlow Lite weights.

Only three classes are supported: early blight, healthy, and late blight.

What the result means

Validation accuracy: 95.68% on 440 images; macro F1: 0.9566. The 424-image test set remains locked and has not been evaluated.

These are controlled dataset results, not a measure of performance on field or drone photos. A high score can still be wrong.

Private by design

Prediction runs in your browser. This app does not upload or store your selected photo. The model downloads when you open the page.
